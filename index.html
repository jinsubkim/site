Short Bio

Jinsub Kim received a Ph.D. degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering with minors in Applied Math and Statistics from Cornell University, where he was advised by Prof. Lang Tong. After a brief postdoc at Cornell (Sep 2013 ~ June 2014), he joined Oregon State University in August 2014. Since March 2019, he has been serving as the chair of the IEEE Signal Processing Society Chapter at IEEE Oregon Section. He served as a co-chair of the Data Analytics and Computation symposium of 2020 IEEE SmartGridComm.
